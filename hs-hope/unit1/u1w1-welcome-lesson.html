01
Section 1 · What This Course Is

So… what is this course?

HOPE stands for Health Opportunities through Physical Education. It's a year-long course that does two things at once: it gets your body moving, and it gives you the real information you need to make smart calls about your health — now and for the rest of your life.

Here's the part that makes this different from a class where you just read and take quizzes: you're going to actually move. You'll plan workouts, do them, log them, and reflect on how they felt. The "PE" in HOPE is real. You bring the effort; the course gives you the game plan.

On the fitness side, you'll test where you're starting from, set goals, and design activity plans you actually follow. On the health side, you'll dig into nutrition, mental health, staying safe online, avoiding substances, healthy relationships, and more. By June, this all adds up to one thing: a plan for staying healthy for life — and your one PE credit toward graduation.

🌎No gym? No problem.

You don't need a gym, fancy equipment, or any sports background to crush this course. Every activity has options for small spaces and no equipment. Wherever you're starting from is the right place to start — we build from there.

The big idea behind everything: HELP

This whole course runs on one simple philosophy, and it spells out a word you'll see all year: HELP. It's the reason we do things the way we do.

❤️
H — Health

🤝
E — Everyone
For every body type, ability, and fitness level — not just athletes. Find what works for you.
♾️
L — Lifetime
We're building habits you'll use at 25, 45, and 75 — not cramming for one fitness test.
🎯
P — Personal
Your plan is yours: your goals, your schedule, your favorite ways to move.

Section 3 · How You'll Work

What "self-paced" really means

This course is online and self-paced. That's a gift and a trap at the same time. The gift: you control when and where you work. The trap: nobody's standing over you, so it's easy to fall behind without noticing.

The honest truth — students who do great in self-paced courses aren't the smartest or the fittest. They're the ones who build a routine. Every week of HOPE follows the same rhythm:

Section 4 · Showing Your Work

How you'll prove you're moving: the activity log

Since this is real PE, a big part of your grade comes from actually being active — not just reading about it. The way you show that is your activity log. Think of it like a workout diary you'll keep all year. Each time you're active, you'll record four things:

🏷️
What you did
Walked, danced, played soccer, lifted, cleaned hard — it all counts.
⏱️
How long
Minutes of activity. Even 10-minute chunks add up.
🔥
How hard
Light, moderate, or vigorous — how much it raised your heart rate.
💬
How it felt
A quick note. This is how you learn what works for you.
