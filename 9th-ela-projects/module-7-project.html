The assignment
 
Trace one thread through The Crucible and at least two earlier course texts, use one researched source, and argue a thesis that answers your own research question.
The essay must contain
 
☐A thread: one of justice, loyalty, family, or duty.
☐Three texts: The Crucible, The Scarlet Letter, and at least one more earlier course text. Quote the play only from your own copy, briefly, citing the act and the page from your edition.
☐One researched source, judged with the four-question credibility check.
☐A counterclaim: one genuine objection to your thesis, stated fairly and answered.
☐MLA throughout, with in-text citations and a Works Cited page.
☐900–1,200 words, not counting the Works Cited page.
