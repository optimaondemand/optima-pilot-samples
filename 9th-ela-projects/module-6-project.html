Module 6 Project: Literary Analysis Essay

100 pointsWord essay, 900 to 1,200 wordsProject PDF
Choose one prompt
 
A. The letter. Hawthorne lets the meaning of the scarlet letter change. What changes it: the community or Hester?
B. The minister. Does Dimmesdale’s public confession redeem him, or does it come too late?
C. The theme. What does a community owe the person it judges, and what does a person owe the truth? Argue what the novel answers.
The essay must
 
☐Length: 900 to 1,200 words, not counting the Works Cited page.
☐State one arguable thesis in the introduction.
☐Quote the novel at least three times, each introduced and cited by chapter, like this: (Hawthorne, ch. 6).
☐Use one researched source beyond the novel, woven into your argument, cited in the text, and listed in Works Cited.
☐Include a counterclaim paragraph with a concession and a rebuttal.
☐Use MLA format: Times New Roman 12 point, double-spaced, last name and page number in the header, a heading block, a title, and a Works Cited page.
