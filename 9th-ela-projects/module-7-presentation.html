Module 7 Presentation
English 1 · Module 7
25 pointsRecorded, 3–5 minutes
Purpose
 
Present your essay’s argument aloud in 3–5 minutes: the thesis, one moment from each text, and what the research added. Slides are optional.
What to cover
 
MinutesContent
0:00–0:45The thesis and the research question it answers.
0:45–2:30One moment from each of the three texts, and the point where they differ.
2:30–3:30What the researched source added.
3:30–5:00The strongest objection, the reply, and what follows if the thesis is right.
Upload
 
Record in the browser or upload a video file. Add slides only if you used them.
How this is scored
 
CriterionStrong (full points)DevelopingBeginning
