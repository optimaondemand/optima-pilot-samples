Your Research Question

1 Thread: justice, loyalty, family, or duty
2 Three texts: The Crucible, The Scarlet Letter, and one more
3 Angle: Salem 1692, the 1950s, or criticism
4 One question that research can settle

Salem, 1692

How do the real events compare with the play? Look for scholarly articles, books, and reference entries on the trials.

The 1950s hearings

How did the period shape the play? Miller wrote it in the 1950s. Look for scholarly articles on McCarthyism and the arts, and period newspapers.

Criticism of an earlier text

What do critics say about a text you will compare to the play? Look for scholarly articles on that text.

Which question is narrow enough to research and open enough to argue?

A model, on a different thread

Thread: honesty. Texts: Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, The Scarlet Letter, The Crucible.

Question: How do critics explain why Hester refuses to name the father of her child, and does the same reasoning explain the choices made by characters in the other two texts?

Your research question One sentence that ends in a question mark. Narrow enough to answer, open enough to argue. Use your thread and at least one of your texts.
  • It is a question.
  • A source could answer it.
  • Informed readers could disagree.
  • It connects to my thread and my texts.

The Ledger

Each row holds one moment, where it is, and what it shows about your thread. Quote The Scarlet Letter and your other earlier text exactly as they appear in your edition. For the play, describe the moment in your own words, or quote one sentence at most from your own copy with the act and the page.

Two model rows, on a different thread
The Crucible, honesty

Moment: Act 4. John Proctor signs a false confession to save his life, then refuses to let the court keep the signed paper, and tears it.

What it shows: He is willing to say a false thing to survive but will not let a false thing be fixed to his name. Honesty is tied to who he is, not only to what he says.

The Scarlet Letter, loyalty

Moment: Ch. III. “I will not speak!” answered Hester.

What it shows: Loyalty here means accepting punishment alone: Hester will not name the father, even when the whole town demands a name.

Your Thesis

TopicLoyalty in three texts.
ObservationCharacters protect people they are loyal to.
ThesisEach text shows a protector who pays for the protection, and each judges the payment differently.

Which is a thesis?

A model, on a different thread

Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, The Scarlet Letter, and The Crucible each show a person who hides a truth to protect someone, and each text suggests that the hiding costs the hider more than the person it was meant to protect.

Your thesis One complex sentence that answers your research question and names all three texts.
  • A reader could disagree.
  • All three texts help prove it.
  • It answers my research question.
