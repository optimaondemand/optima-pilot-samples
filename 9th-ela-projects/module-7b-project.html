The assignment
 
Put one American document from this module into genuine conversation with one work of literature you read this year, use four researched sources to support the argument, and answer a question you chose yourself.
The paper must contain
 
RequirementSpecifically
A question you choseStated as a question in your introduction, and answered by your thesis.
One document from this moduleThe Declaration, the Preamble or Bill of Rights, Federalist No. 10, the Gettysburg Address, Lincoln’s Second Inaugural, Washington’s Farewell, or the Four Freedoms. For King’s letter, use your own licensed copy and quote briefly.
One literary work from this yearElectra, Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, The Canterbury Tales, Shakespeare’s sonnets, a short story, or The Scarlet Letter.
Four researched sourcesAt least one scholarly source from JSTOR; at least two primary sources; no more than one general reference source. These are in addition to your two anchor texts.
A counterargumentOne genuine objection to your reading, stated in a form its holder would accept, and answered.
MLA throughoutIn-text citations for everything borrowed, and a Works Cited page. The page does not count toward your word total.
A structure that works
 
You are not required to use this shape, but a paper that departs from it should do so on purpose.
SectionRoughlyWhat happens
Introduction150–200 wordsName both texts and pose your question. End with your thesis.
Body × 3 or 4700–900 wordsOne idea per paragraph, with both texts inside it. Not a paragraph per text. Research sources appear here, doing work your own reading cannot do alone.
Counterargument150–200 wordsThe strongest objection, fairly stated, then your reply. Placing it late works well; placing it nowhere costs you the top band.
Conclusion100–150 wordsWhat changes if you are right. Not a summary of what you just said.
The cover test
 
Cover one of your two texts. If a body paragraph still makes its point, that paragraph has not synthesized anything. It has written about one text and mentioned the other. Run this check on every body paragraph before you submit.

How this is scored
 
CriterionStrongDevelopingBeginning
Argument and synthesis
(30 pts)		A clear, arguable thesis that could only be written by someone holding both texts at once. Every body paragraph uses both. The counterargument is real and fairly stated.A thesis is present and defended, but some paragraphs handle one text at a time, or the counterargument is a weak version of the objection.The paper summarizes each text in turn and asserts a connection, usually in the conclusion.
Use of research
(25 pts)		Four sources meeting the required mix, each doing work the anchor texts cannot. Sources are weighed, not just quoted.The required sources appear and are relevant, but mostly confirm points already made.Sources are missing, do not meet the required mix, or are dropped in without being used.
Evidence and reasoning
(25 pts)		Specific, well-chosen quotations, integrated into your own sentences, each followed by your explanation of what it shows.Adequate evidence, but some quotations are long, dropped in, or left to speak for themselves.Evidence is thin, inaccurate, or unconnected to the claims it is meant to support.
Craft and citation
(20 pts)		Controlled sentences, deliberate structure, and MLA correct in text and on the Works Cited page.Generally clear with some awkwardness; citation is present but inconsistent.Errors interfere with meaning, or citation is largely absent.
On citation and honesty
 
Every source you cite must be one you opened. Every quotation must be accurate. Every idea that is not yours must be attributed, including one you put into your own words. If a source does not exist or a quotation cannot be found in it, that is not a formatting problem. It is handled as any other false statement in academic work.
