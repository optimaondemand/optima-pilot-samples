WEEK 27 · QUARTER 3 CAPSTONE
Week 27 Quarter 3 Capstone
16 points · essay · rubric below
The task

For nine weeks you have followed what happened after Texas joined the Union. This essay asks you to explain, in your own words and with your own evidence, what Texas had to settle once it belonged to the United States.

The prompt

Annexation settled who would own Texas, but not what belonging to the Union would require. Using at least three pieces of evidence from the quarter, explain what Texas had to settle after 1845, and how each thing was settled.

Directions
1
Look back over Weeks 19 to 27 and your notes.
2
Choose at least three specific pieces of evidence from at least three different weeks. Evidence can come from any lesson, for example the war with Mexico and the treaty that fixed the border, secession and Reconstruction, the Constitution of 1876, the closing of the Comanche frontier, or the cattle kingdom and the railroads.
3
Write a five-paragraph essay of about 500 words: an opening paragraph that states your claim, three body paragraphs that each develop one piece of evidence, and a closing paragraph that ties them into one explanation.
4
Type your essay in the box below and submit it. Use your own words.
Success criteria
☐ My opening paragraph states a clear claim.
☐ I used at least three pieces of evidence from at least three different weeks.
☐ Each body paragraph names the event, person, or law and explains how it was settled.
☐ My closing paragraph ties the evidence into one explanation.
☐ I wrote it in my own words.
