The Door Opens

The Metamorphosis · the end of Part I
Header: AI-generated image
Focus

Gregor means one thing, and the room perceives another. What happens when a story lets us hear a mind that no one in the scene can?

Objectives
  • Describe the gap between what Gregor intends and what the others perceive at the door.
  • Compare Gregor’s reception with the creature’s in Frankenstein.
  • Tell direct thought, indirect thought, and free indirect discourse apart.
  • Write free indirect sentences about Part I.

B

Three Readings

What does keeping the narration inside Gregor’s mind do in the door scene?

The father weeps when the door opens, then drives Gregor back. What best explains the change?

The father drives Gregor back with the clerk’s stick and a newspaper, stamping and hissing. What does that show about how he now treats Gregor?

Where we are
