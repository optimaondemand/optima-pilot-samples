Your Argument

The question

Who made Gregor what he is: the change, the firm, or the family?

Directions
  1. Position. Name it in your first sentence: the one you hold most responsible, or how you divide the blame among the three.
  2. Two moments. Describe two moments from Part I by what happens and where, such as his worry about the missed train, the chief clerk’s visit, or the father driving him back into his room. Quote a few words from your copy only if they carry the point, and name the part.
  3. The other side. State the strongest case for a different answer fairly, then answer it.
  4. His thought. Write one sentence that gives Gregor’s thought in the narrator’s voice, with no tag such as “he thought.”
  5. Word Study. Use at least two of the week’s words, accurately.
