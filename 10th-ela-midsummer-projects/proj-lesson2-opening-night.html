Opening Night

Module 3 major project
Header: AI-generated image
Focus

What must a theatergoer see on your design page, and what must a cast hear in your call, for your reading of the scene to arrive?

Objectives
  • Plan a design page: a setting and a costume or colour concept, each with a caption, a credit, alt text and a one-line rationale.
  • Write a cast list with a one-line note for each character.
  • Write a call script of clear, ordered instructions with one planned pause.
  • Assemble the program in order and export it as a PDF.

Plan the Page

Plan for your own scene. Quote an exact line from the play in each rationale and cite it like this: (1.1).

Setting concept Where the scene happens and how it looks, then one line of rationale tied to a quoted line. 2–4 sentences.
Costume or colour concept What the characters wear, or the colours that run through the scene, then one line of rationale tied to a quoted line. 2–4 sentences.
B

Plan the Images

Each image comes from one of four places: your own drawing, a photo you took, a public-domain image with its credit, or an AI image that is labelled.

You use an AI image on your design page. What must sit beside it?

Setting image Which of the four sources, who made it, and your caption. If it is AI, add the sentence on what it got wrong.
Costume or colour image Which of the four sources, who made it, and your caption. If it is AI, add the sentence on what it got wrong.
Alt text One sentence for each image, for a reader who cannot see it. Say what is in it, not what you hope it means. Label them Setting and Costume.
C

The Cast List

One line for every character who speaks or appears in your scene.

Cast list Name, then one line that tells the reader who this person is in your production. Check each name against the play.
D

AI and Your Program

An AI tool can write a director’s note in seconds. It can also misstate the play, invent lines, and describe scenes that are not in the text, so check every claim against the play.

An AI concept image may appear on the design page only if it is labelled as AI generated and followed by a sentence critiquing what it got wrong.

If you ask an AI tool for ideas, keep a short note of what you asked and what you checked yourself, and include it with your program. Using AI is optional.

B

Write Your Call Script

Write it the way you will say it. Start each instruction with a verb.

The key moment Quote the line or lines the moment turns on, exactly as printed, and cite them: (1.1). Then say in one sentence what the moment is.
Your instructions 6–10, numbered, one action each, in the order the cast will do them. Use precise verbs: stop, turn, cross, lower, hold.
Your planned pause Where it falls, how long it lasts, and why the moment needs it. 1–2 sentences.
C

Rehearse and Record

Before you record
  • I read my script aloud once, start to finish.
  • Every instruction starts with a verb and asks for one action.
  • My instructions are in the order the cast will do them.
  • I marked where my pause falls and how long it lasts.
  • I timed a read: between 2 and 3 minutes.
The recording
  • Use any device: a phone, a tablet, a computer webcam or a school recording tool. Audio or video is accepted.
  • Aim for 2 to 3 minutes.
  • Save the file as LastName_FirstName_PlayersCompanyCall_ELA10. If your tool gives you a link instead of a file, keep the link.
A

The Program, in Order

  1. CoverTitle of your scene, your name, and the act and scene.
  2. The Director’s Essay700 to 1,000 words, under headings a theatergoer can scan.
  3. The Design PageSetting and costume or colour, each with its image, caption, credit and rationale line.
  4. The Cast ListEvery character in your scene, one line each.
  5. One Cited PassageA passage from the play, exactly as printed, with its act and scene.
B

Check the Pages

  • Every section has a heading a reader can find.
  • Every image has a caption beneath it and a credit.
  • Every image has alt text. In Word: right-click the image, then Edit Alt Text. In PowerPoint: right-click, then View Alt Text.
  • Any AI image is labelled, with a sentence on what it got wrong.
  • Every quotation matches the play word for word and is cited by act and scene.
  • Pages run in the order above, with no blank pages.
C

Export to PDF

Build in your school Microsoft 365 account, and save to Optima, then ELA, then ELA10 in OneDrive.

  2. In the file type list, pick PDF.
  3. Name it LastName_FirstName_PlayersCompany_ELA10.pdf and save.

In the browser version: File, then Export, then Download as PDF. Rename the download to the name above.

From PowerPoint
  1. Choose File, then Export, then Create PDF/XPS Document.
  2. Or choose Save As, and pick PDF as the file type.
  3. Name it LastName_FirstName_PlayersCompany_ELA10.pdf and save.
