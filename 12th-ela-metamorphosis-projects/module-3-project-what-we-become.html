What this project is
 
The question: What makes a monster, and what makes us human?
What you makeOne finished work in the format you chose in Week 1: Analytical or Synthesis Essay, Debate, Letters or Diary, Short Film or Screenplay, or Podcast or Oration. It argues a specific answer that a reasonable reader could dispute.
Texts you useThe Metamorphosis and at least two other module texts: “Miss Brill,” “Araby,” “Self-Reliance,” Meditations, “The Collar,” “The Tyger,” or “Hope is the thing with feathers.” Frankenstein may support your argument but does not count as one of the two.
Sources you findTwo scholarly sources from JStor: one on Kafka or The Metamorphosis, one on another module text or on the idea of the monstrous and the human. Cite both in MLA 9.
DueEnd of Week 9. Only the finished project and its process record are scored.

Worth knowing: AI and your project
 
An AI tool can suggest sentence structure and point out a loose paragraph in work you give it. It can misstate what a text says, invent quotations, and invent sources that do not exist, so check every quotation against the text and every source on JStor. It cannot decide whether your interpretation is right. The claim, the choices, and the voice are yours, and you should be ready to explain every part. Using AI is optional. If you do, keep a short note of what you asked and what you checked yourself.
