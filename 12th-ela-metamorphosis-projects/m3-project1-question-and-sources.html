Your Working Claim

Where you stood in Week 1 · your verdict
1 · Your question Narrow the module question until it names your texts and one pressure point. One sentence.
2 · Your working claim Your answer, as a position a reader could reject. 2–3 sentences. It can change; it cannot be blank.
Test it
  • A careful reader could disagree with it.
  • It names what I mean by monster or by human.
  • My three texts can all speak to it.
  • It is more than a fact and narrower than “everyone.”
3 · What would change your mind? One kind of evidence or one reading that would force you to revise the claim. 2–3 sentences.
