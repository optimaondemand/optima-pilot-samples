Frame, Quote, Explain

Frame

Emerson makes self-trust a duty, not a mood, when he writes,

Quote

“Nothing is at last sacred but the integrity of your own mind” (Emerson).

Explain

The word sacred lifts one’s own mind above family, firm, and custom, the three authorities Gregor resents in private and goes on serving.

The explanation does the analytical work. It names the word that matters and says what it does for your claim. Which one explains?

A student frames and quotes Marcus: Marcus holds that every upset is a judgment we add, since “all is but opinion and conceit.” Which sentence should come next?

C

Quote, Paraphrase, Summary

Quote

“Whoso would be a man must be a nonconformist” (Emerson).

Use when the exact words are the evidence, and you will comment on a word or a rhythm.

Paraphrase

Emerson says that anyone who wants to be fully adult must be willing to break from the group (Emerson).

Use when the idea matters and the wording does not. Same length, your words, still cited.

Summary

Emerson argues that maturity means refusing conformity (Emerson).

Use to carry a whole passage or article in a sentence or two, when you need its direction and not its detail.

A JStor article spends several pages building the case that a family can become the monster it fears. You need that whole case in one sentence of your second move. What do you write?
