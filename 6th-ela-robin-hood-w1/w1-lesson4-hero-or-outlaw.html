An AI-generated character and voice.
Three passes through your draft
  1. Paragraphs. Each one answers its question: who your hero is and what they did, the hero quality, the rule-breaking question.
  2. Feeling words. Swap nice or caring for something your hero did.
  3. Capitals. Every name of a person, place, or group gets one. Plain nouns do not.
150–200 words in all · 2 to 3 short paragraphs
