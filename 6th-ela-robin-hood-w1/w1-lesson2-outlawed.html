✏️ Daily Oral Language
Study the model, then fix the sentence.
✅ Correct model
Prince John and the steward Worman plotted to seize the lands of Locksley.

Prince John, Worman, and Locksley are names, so they take capitals. Steward and lands are plain words. Prince is capitalized here because it is part of one person’s name.

🔧 Fix this one
at fountains abbey, sir guy of gisborne stopped the wedding of robin and marian.
Rewrite it correctly.
🔍 Check my work

At Fountains Abbey, Sir Guy of Gisborne stopped the wedding of Robin and Marian. Fountains Abbey is one named place. Sir Guy of Gisborne is a full title and name, so Sir, Guy, and Gisborne are capitalized and the small word of is not. Wedding stays lowercase.

🔍 Mini-Research: Is There a Historical Robin Hood?
Find two reliable sources

Green’s Robin is a character in a book. The legend is older, and people still argue about whether a real outlaw stood behind the name. Your question:

🎯 Research question

Is there a real, historical person behind the Robin Hood legend, and how do reliable sources answer that question?

Find two reliable sources. Reliable means an encyclopedia article (such as Britannica or Britannica Kids), a museum or university website, or a library database article. A random blog, forum post, fan wiki, or AI chatbot answer does not count. If you are not sure about a source, ask your teacher.

For each source, record who published it, the title of the page, and what it says about whether Robin Hood was real.
