⚖️ Claim or List?
Daniel, Prospero, and Gareth all give things up. In these stories, the hero is the one who stands up when everyone else stays seated. Midas loves gold, and then he loves Marygold. St. George, Robin Hood, and Benedick show that heroism often means standing up for someone everyone else has given up on. St. George, Robin Hood, and Benedick are all heroes. A duty is easy to keep until it costs something, and these characters keep it anyway. Midas, Beatrice, and Daniel are happy at the end. Daniel, Prospero, and Gareth show that what a person gives up for others can come back as something better. King Arthur is brave, and Sir Gawain is brave too. Prospero buries his books, and Gareth works in a kitchen. Sir Richard, Beatrice, and Daniel all do their duty. St. George has a lot of duties on his estate. Giving something up only counts as a sacrifice when the person could have kept it. These characters learn that happiness needs other people more than it needs power or riches. Sir Richard, Beatrice, and Daniel show that a real duty is owed to a person, and paying it always costs something. Midas, Beatrice, and Daniel find happiness only after they let go of something they held tight: gold, pride, and hatred.

📌 One way a topic sentence can sound

Across three very different stories, heroism takes the same shape: a person faces something that could destroy them and moves toward it anyway, not because they are unafraid, but because the call in front of them matters more than their fear.

Notice: it names the idea and says what shape it takes. Yours names your three characters.

Jonah’s part
Jonah · 6th Grade Scholar

In “The Tempest” by Charles and Mary Lamb, Gonzalo is a lord at Prospero’s court. When Antonio puts Prospero and little Miranda out to sea, Gonzalo has already “privately placed in the boat, water, provisions, apparel, and some books” (Lamb, “The Tempest”). He does it secretly, because the new duke wants them gone. Twelve years later, Prospero calls him “the preserver of his life.”

Notice: the book and author, the moment, then why it matters. Only Lamb and Hawthorne may be quoted; tell the other books in your own words.
