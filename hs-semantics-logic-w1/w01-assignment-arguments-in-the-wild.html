What you need

  • Something you read or heard this week: a news article, an advertisement, a conversation, a video, or a textbook page.
  • Your notes from Lessons 1.01a, 1.01b, and 1.01c.

Find three statements from your own reading or listening this week: one argument, one assertion or opinion, and one explanation. Copy each one exactly and say where it came from. Then explain what makes it belong in its category.

Argument

One statement is offered to support another.

Assertion or opinion

A claim with no support, or a statement of preference.

Explanation

The cause of a fact everyone already accepts.
