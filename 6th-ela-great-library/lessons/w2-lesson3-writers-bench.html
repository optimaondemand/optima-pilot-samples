✏️ Grammar: Learn
Step 1 of 3 this week: verbs and tense.

A verb shows an action or a way of being. The tense of a verb tells when: it happened before now, it is happening now, or it has not happened yet.

WordWhat it doesExample
verbshows action or beingrode, swore, was
past tensealready happenedGawain rode.
present tensehappening nowGawain rides.
future tensehas not happened yetGawain will ride.
steady tenseone time all through a passageGawain took the lace and hid it.
strong verban exact verb in place of went, got, did, had, or wasgalloped, strode, crept

Priya’s paragraph
6th Grade Scholar

Sir Gawain kept the hardest promise in the chapter. When the squire begged him to flee and swore he would never tell, Gawain refused and rode down to the Green Chapel alone. Yet he broke a smaller promise. On the third evening he gave the lord three kisses but hid the green lace, because he feared for his life. When the Green Knight named the lace, Gawain handed it back and said, “I am ashamed.” He made no excuse and blamed no one but himself. Gawain’s story showed that a good knight was not a knight with no faults, but a knight who told the truth about them.
