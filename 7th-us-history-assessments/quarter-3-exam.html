QUARTER 3 · EXAM
Quarter 3 Exam: A Republic Divides Over What It Is Building
27 questions · 27 points · 1 attempt
Before you start

This exam covers Quarter 3 only: the Founding Era through the annexation of Texas and the Mexican War. It has 27 multiple-choice questions, one point each, and should take about 40 minutes. Read every question and all four answers before you choose. Many questions ask you to put two or more facts together, so think before you answer.
