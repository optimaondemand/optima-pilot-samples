WEEK 20 · CRITICAL QUESTIONS
Critical Questions: Jefferson's First Inaugural Address (1801)
10 points · written answers · one turn-in
Instructions

Read the primary source for this week: Thomas Jefferson's First Inaugural Address. Then answer both questions in your own words. Aim for four to six sentences each.

  1. Jefferson spoke to a country that had just come through a tie, 36 votes, and talk of force. Suppose his speech had attacked the Federalists as enemies of the republic instead of calling them fellow citizens. What might have happened next, and why?
  2. Why would later Americans care about a speech in which the winner of a bitter election speaks to the losers as part of the same nation? Explain why it matters beyond the year 1801.

How to submit: type both answers in the text box, numbering them 1 and 2. This assignment is graded on effort and thinking: a complete, thoughtful answer to both questions earns full credit.
