WEEK 20 · PRIMARY SOURCE REPORT
Primary Source Report: Marshall in Marbury v. Madison (1803)
16 points · Word document · one turn-in
Instructions

Read the opinion excerpt from Marbury v. Madison. Then write a response of 250 to 400 words in a Word document that answers both questions below. Use your own words and refer to the source and to the lessons.

  1. Suppose Marshall had ordered Madison to deliver Marbury's commission. What would Jefferson likely have done, and what would that have meant for the Court?
  2. Why does the power Marshall claimed in this opinion matter to ordinary Americans today, not just to the government in 1803?

Your teacher will score your report with the rubric attached to this assignment: understanding of the material, quality of reasoning, clarity and precision of expression, and intellectual honesty. Intellectual honesty means telling what the source cannot show or what you are still unsure about.
