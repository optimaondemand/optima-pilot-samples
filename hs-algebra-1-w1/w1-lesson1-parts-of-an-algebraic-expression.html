All the words in this lesson
ExpressionA combination of numbers, variables, and operations, such as 0.5x2 + 7y − 3. It has no equals sign.
TermA single number, a variable, or numbers and variables multiplied together. In 3x + 2, the terms are 3x and 2.
CoefficientThe number multiplying the variable in a term. In 7x2, the coefficient is 7.
VariableA symbol, usually a letter, that stands for a number we don’t know yet.
ExponentA small number to the upper right of a base. It shows how many times the base is multiplied by itself.
ConstantA term with no variable. Its value doesn’t change. In 3x + 2, the constant is 2.
PolynomialAn expression of one or more terms built from variables and coefficients using addition, subtraction, multiplication, and whole-number exponents. Examples: 7h3, 4t2 − 3r, x2y + 7x − 31y.
MonomialA polynomial with one term: a number (5), a variable (x), or a number times variables (3x2y).
BinomialA polynomial with exactly two terms, such as 3x + 2 or 43z2 − 37y.
TrinomialA polynomial with exactly three terms, such as x2y + 7x − 31y.
FactorFactoring a polynomial means finding expressions that multiply together to make it. The factors of x2 − x − 6 are (x − 3) and (x + 2).
Linear functionA function whose graph is a straight line, written f(x) = mx + b. The slope m is the rate of change; b is where the line crosses the y-axis.
Quadratic functionA second-degree polynomial function, f(x) = ax2 + bx + c with a ≠ 0. Its graph is a parabola that opens up or down depending on the sign of a.
Exponential functionA function f(x) = a · bx, where the base b is positive and not 1, and the variable is the exponent. It shows growth or decay.

Expression or Equation?

An expression names a quantity: 0.5x2 + 7y − 3. An equation says two expressions are equal, so it has an equals sign: 7y − 9x = 15.
