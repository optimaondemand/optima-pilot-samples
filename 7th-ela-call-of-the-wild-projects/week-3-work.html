Quick Write 3: Prove It: Your Evidence Paragraph
 
Take a position on the big question: Does Buck's transformation set him free, or destroy who he was? Write one paragraph of 100–150 words that supports it: a claim about how Buck changes in Chapter III, one exact line from Chapter III introduced and cited, and two or three sentences of explanation that answer "so what?" No fragments, no run-ons.
