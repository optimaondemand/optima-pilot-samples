Quick Write 4: Answer the Other Side, Then Conclude
 
Write the claim you have been building at the top. Then write a counterclaim of two to three sentences: the strongest fair point someone could make against your claim, answered with a detail from Chapter IV or V. Then write a conclusion of two to three sentences that restates your claim in fresh words and adds no new idea. 100–180 words.
