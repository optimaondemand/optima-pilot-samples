Quick Write 5: Your Essay Outline and Opening Paragraph
 
Build the outline of your argumentative essay on the question Does Buck's transformation set him free, or destroy who he was? in four parts (introduction and thesis · evidence and explanation · counterclaim and response · conclusion), giving Buck's love for John Thornton a place. Then write your introduction as one paragraph of 4 to 6 sentences: a hook, one or two sentences of background, and your thesis last.
