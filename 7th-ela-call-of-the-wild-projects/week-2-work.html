Quick Write 2: Argument step 1: thesis + one reason
 
Answer the focus question, Does Buck's transformation set him free, or destroy who he was?, in one thesis sentence someone could argue with. Then give one reason in two or three sentences, built on one moment from Chapter II, quoted or named and cited (London, Ch. 2). 50–100 words.
