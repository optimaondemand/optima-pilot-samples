More tips: choosing well

This is the end of the whole story and the reading gets heavier. Both tales are about knights failing, which is why they are the most useful passages in the book for your essay.

Launcelot turned back from the Grail. The greatest knight in arms does not get there. Read the moment and the reason together.

Galahad, Percivale, and Bors at the end of the quest, where the book explains what the three of them had that the others did not.

The fellowship dividing. Loyalty to Arthur against loyalty to Launcelot, with no way to keep both.

Bedivere and Excalibur. He is told to throw the sword into the water and he does not, twice. Then he does. Reading his three trips to the water aloud makes the delay do something no summary of it can.

Pick a passage about a knight you plan to write about. You are choosing evidence for your essay and reading it closely at the same time.
