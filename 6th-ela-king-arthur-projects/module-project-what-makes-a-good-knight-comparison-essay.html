Quality of reasoning
a clear, debatable claim about what makes a good knight, a comparison of the two knights' actions and principles, the opposing reading fairly weighed, and an early, middle and late moment per knight.		Excellent (30 pts): Claim is sharp and earned; comparison drives the essay; opposing reading stated fairly, then answered; 3 well-placed moments per knight.Proficient (22 pts): Clear claim and comparison; some weighing of alternatives; evidence present for each knight.Developing (15 pts): Claim present but thin; comparison leans toward summary; uneven evidence.Beginning (8 pts): Summary rather than argument; no real claim or comparison.30 pts
Clarity & precision of expression
clear intro/body/conclusion, smooth transitions, varied sentences, correctly embedded quotations, clean mechanics; Heading styles.
