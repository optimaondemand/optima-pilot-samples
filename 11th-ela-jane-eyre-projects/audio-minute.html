Chronicle Audio Minute
English 3 · Module 3 · Jane Eyre
10 points2 to 3 minutes, one takeAudio file or linkDue at the end of Week 7
What you make
 
You record one conversational audio segment, 2 to 3 minutes long, that explains one feature of Jane’s world to a listener who has never read the novel. It is one take, lightly scripted, with no editing and no music, recorded on any device.
Focus question
 
If a listener has never opened Jane Eyre, can you explain one part of her world clearly enough in two to three minutes that they could say it back to someone else?
Your segment includes
 
›Three or four talking points. Short phrases to speak from, not sentences to read out.
›Two terms a stranger needs, each defined in plain words as it comes up.
›An opening line that gives a stranger a reason to keep listening.
›The same angle as your Chronicle feature: the position of a Victorian governess, the Gothic mode, inheritance, marriage law, and money, or the shape of a coming-of-age story.
Steps
 
1Choose one feature of Jane’s world inside the angle you picked for your Chronicle feature.
2Write three or four talking points as short phrases.
3Pick two terms a stranger would need, and write a plain definition of each to say as the term comes up.
4Write your opening line.
5Rehearse once aloud from the talking points only. If you catch yourself reading, put the page down.
6Record one take of 2 to 3 minutes on any device. Do not edit it and do not add music.
7Save the file as LastName_FirstName_AudioMinute_ELA11 and submit it here, or submit a working link to it, such as a OneDrive share link.
Done when

Worth knowing: AI and your recording
 
An AI tool can write a fluent script and still get the facts wrong, or misquote the novel. Your talking points, your definitions, and your voice have to be yours. Using AI is optional.
