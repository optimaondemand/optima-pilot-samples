Professor Lovelore

Well done. Now look at what those three moments show you. Arthur rushes to save Merlin. Then his pride leads him into a fight he cannot win, and his sword breaks. And at the lake, he gives his word before he takes Excalibur.

In these moments, Green does not tell you what Arthur is like. He shows you, through what Arthur does. That is characterization. Watch what a person does, and you will learn who they are.
