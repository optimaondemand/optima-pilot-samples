✏️ Grammar: Nouns, Pronouns, Antecedents
Step 1 of 3 this week: learn the words.

A noun names a person, place, thing, or idea: Merlin, Camelot, sword, honor. A pronoun takes the place of a noun so you do not have to repeat it: he, she, it, they, him, his, their. The noun a pronoun points back to is its antecedent.

Every pronoun needs an antecedent a reader can find. Ask of each one: which noun is this standing in for?

WordWhat it doesExample
nounnames somethingMerlin, Camelot, sword
pronounstands in for a nounhe, they, it, his
antecedentthe noun the pronoun points back toThe Lady of the Lake rose from the water, and she held out a sword. (she → Lady of the Lake)
