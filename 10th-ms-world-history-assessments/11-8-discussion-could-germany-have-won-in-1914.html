HOW TO WORK
Read the two lessons and the two primary sources first, and look over your notes in My Notebook. Then post your answer to the prompt below, and reply to one classmate.
DISCUSSION PROMPT

This week’s question: Could Germany have won in 1914?

Choose a side. In your answer, name at least two specific decisions made between 1905 and September 1914, and explain how each one could have gone another way.

Write 150 to 250 words. State one claim you believe is true. Then explain why, using the specific evidence named above. Keep to what the lessons and the sources show. If you are guessing, say so.

Then add one sentence on the moral side of the question. The plan required marching through a neutral country that the great powers had promised to protect. Does that bear on whether it was a good plan? Say what you think and why.

RESPOND TO A CLASSMATE

Reply to one classmate. Do you agree with their reasoning? If so, strengthen it with a fact or decision they did not use. If not, say politely where you think it falls short, and give your reason.
