The question
 
When the structures of civilization fall away, what is left of us: the better part or the worse?
Answer it through the novel. The worse part may win, the better part may survive in some boys, or what is left may depend on the person. Any of these can earn full marks if defended well.
The essay must
 
☐State a clear thesis in the introduction: one sentence that answers the central question and can be defended.
☐Prove it with evidence from the text: at least three specific moments or short quotations, each explained in your own words. Quote only from your own copy of the novel.
☐State and answer at least two strong objections. For each, name the best case a thoughtful reader could make against you, then answer it honestly.
