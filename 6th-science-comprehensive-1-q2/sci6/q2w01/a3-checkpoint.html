Safety and time

All seven use household materials and none needs heat. Ask an adult before you start, work on a surface that can get wet, keep food coloring off clothes, and do not eat or drink any of the materials. You have the entire quarter to complete your experiment and final video project. Some projects take time. Do not wait until the last second.
