The Mechanicals Cast a Play

Act 1, Scene 2
Header: AI-generated image
Focus

Six workmen cast a play to perform at the duke’s wedding, and one of them wants every part. What do the way they speak and what they ask for show about them?

Objectives
  • Tell blank verse, rhymed couplets, and prose apart, and say who speaks each.
  • Mark the beats of two lines and find where one of them bends the pattern.
  • Name the parts Quince assigns and the player for each.
  • Explain what Bottom’s requests show about him, using exact lines.
  • Explain what the workmen fear and what that fear says about the court.

Court and Workmen

Scene 1 is spoken in verse, in the duke’s palace. Scene 2 opens in prose, with six workmen. What does the change of speech tell an audience first?

Bottom Wants Every Part

Flute objects to playing a woman: “I have a beard coming.” Bottom answers, “And I may hide my face, let me play Thisbe too.” What is he doing?

Bottom asks for the lion as well: “Let me play the lion too.” Why does Quince refuse?
