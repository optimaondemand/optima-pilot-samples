What a Script Fixes

The stage direction

Enter Egeus, Hermia, Lysander and Demetrius.

The text fixes
  • Four people come on: Egeus, Hermia, Lysander and Demetrius.
  • Egeus speaks first, and his first words are “Happy be Theseus, our renownèd Duke!”
The director decides
  • Whether they enter together or in pairs, and how fast.
  • Whether Egeus holds Hermia’s arm.
  • Who looks at whom while the duke greets them.
Egeus’s complaint
EgeusFull of vexation come I, with complaint
Against my child, my daughter Hermia.(1.1)
The text fixes
  • What he says, word for word.
  • That the complaint is about his own child.
The director decides
  • How loud, and how fast.
  • Where he stands.
  • Where he stops to breathe.
