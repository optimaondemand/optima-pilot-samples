QUESTION 1
A student claims that Mississippi left the Union mainly over tariffs. Which feature of the Declaration of Causes would best challenge that claim?
A. Its long discussion of the tariff of 1828.
B. Its request for help from foreign governments.
C. Its silence about every issue except taxes.
D. Its central argument is about slavery and the danger the delegates saw to it.
QUESTION 2

A. A statement, in the delegates' own words, of why they acted.
B. The legal effect of the vote to secede.
C. Any record that a convention was held.
D. The names of the delegates who attended.
QUESTION 3

A. Mississippians had already agreed on secession for decades.
B. The convention had no real power to decide the question.
C. The delegates were chosen from only one party.
D. Leaving the Union was a contested choice, not a unanimous one.
QUESTION 4
