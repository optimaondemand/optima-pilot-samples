WEEK 19 · PRIMARY SOURCE EXERCISE
Primary Source Exercise: Palmer, "The Case Against the 'Reds'"
20 points · written answers · one turn-in
Instructions

Read the Palmer article, then answer all four questions. Write three to five sentences for each. Use evidence from the article and from Lesson 1.4. A strong answer gives a reason, not just an opinion.

QUESTION 1

Palmer wrote in 1920 as if revolution had been close. What events of 1919 made his warning believable to many Americans, even those who had never met a radical?

QUESTION 2

Palmer defends the raids as the way to protect the country. Which principle about how a republic should treat people it accuses of crimes is missing from his defense? Use an example from the raids to show why that principle matters.

QUESTION 3

Palmer predicted violence for May 1, 1920, and nothing happened. Suppose instead that bombs had gone off in several cities that day. How would the Red Scare, and Palmer's reputation, probably have been different? Explain.

QUESTION 4

Palmer wrote to persuade readers, not only to inform them. What in the article shows this? Then name one kind of evidence from outside the article that you would want to check before trusting his claims.
