WEEK 19 · CRITICAL QUESTIONS
Critical Questions: Lodge, Speech Against the League of Nations
20 points · written answers · one turn-in
Instructions

Read the Lodge speech, then answer all four questions. Write three to five sentences for each. Use evidence from the speech and from Lesson 1.2. A strong answer gives a reason, not just an opinion. If your answer depends on something you cannot know, say so.

QUESTION 1

Suppose Wilson had accepted Lodge's change to Article 10 in the fall of 1919, and the Senate had approved the League with that change. Would the League have been stronger or weaker with the United States as a member under those conditions? Explain your reasoning.

QUESTION 2

Lodge was not one of the senators who opposed the League on any terms, yet his objection to Article 10 persuaded many senators who were. Why did an argument about one article carry so much weight in the Senate?

QUESTION 3

Lodge's argument rests on an idea about who should hold the power to commit a nation to war. What is that idea, and why would a citizen of a republic care about it?

QUESTION 4

Suppose the Senate had approved the League without any changes. Would the United States have been better or worse off in the 1920s? Defend your answer with at least one specific consequence.
