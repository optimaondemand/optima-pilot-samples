The question

Who was more responsible for the failure of the League of Nations in the United States: President Wilson or the Senate? Both played a part. Your job is to decide which mattered more.

Your post

Your post (about 100 to 150 words):

  1. Take a clear position: Wilson, the Senate, or neither one more than the other.
  2. Give one specific piece of evidence from Lesson 1.2 or the Lodge speech that supports you.
  3. Name one fact that cuts against your position, and explain why you still hold it.
Your reply

Your reply (about 50 to 75 words): Respond to one classmate. Do you agree with their reasoning? If so, strengthen it with something they did not use. If not, say respectfully where their reasoning falls short.

Before you post

Post first, then reply. You will not see your classmates' posts until you have posted.
