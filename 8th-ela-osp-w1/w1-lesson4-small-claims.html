Who: Tess, a mapmaker sent alone to survey an ice moon, who trusts what she can measure.

What: Forty tall pillars of frost on a ridge, every one turned toward the same dark edge of the sky.

Where: An ice moon where the snow squeaks instead of crunching and something glows blue far under the ice.

The question I want my reader to feel is whether measuring something is the same as understanding it.
