✅ Correct model
The golden light made Ransom vigilant, and Weston called the planet Malacandra.

Two complete thoughts are joined by and, so a comma comes before it. In the first, Ransom is the object and vigilant describes him: an adjective complement. In the second, planet is the object and Malacandra renames it: a noun complement. Both names take capitals.

2
✏️ Step 1 · Learn
Tap the object complement.

An object complement comes right after a direct object and finishes the thought about it. A noun complement renames the object. An adjective complement describes what the object has become. Two quick tests tell them apart.

PartJobExample
subject, verbwho acts, and what they doThe crew elected
direct objectwho or what receives the actionRansom
object complementrenames the object (noun) or tells what it became (adjective)captain · vigilant
TestIf it passes
The object is the ___.noun complement
The object is (or became) ___.adjective complement

Jonah’s draft
Jonah · 8th Grade Scholar

Lewis makes the cabin feel real by choosing details you can feel on your own skin. The hot wall makes Ransom think he is behind a furnace, and the walls look like the sides of a wheelbarrow but feel straight when he touches them, so we learn the ship the way he does, by guessing wrong first. Those details make his fear believable because his body proves it before his mind does. Lewis never explains how the ship works; Weston says Ransom could not understand it. That gap keeps the ship mysterious and makes Weston sound as if he is hiding something. The golden light made the breakfast room dazzling, and that one adjective tells us space is not the cold dark Ransom expected.
