What happens when one man leaves the line?

Achilles won his glory alone. The polis asked for a different kind of courage.

Build a line of hoplites, then send one man charging ahead. Your photo shows what his glory costs the men he left.

  • A hoplite was a Greek citizen who fought as a heavily armed foot soldier. Many were farmers who owned enough land to pay for their own gear.
  • A phalanx was a tight block of men standing shoulder to shoulder, rows deep.
  • Each man’s shield covered his own left side and part of his neighbor’s. Each man had to trust the man beside him.
  • If one soldier ran, the whole line could break. Glory belonged to the whole line.

In VR

Step 2 of 7Go in

First time only: connect Canvas at xr.optimaed.com on a computer. The steps are in Having trouble in VR? below.

  1. Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app.
  2. Click Explore. Type Olympus in the Filter environments box.
  3. Click Mount Olympus, then click Begin Solo.

Step 3 of 7Build the line

Set five Greek Mercenary Standing models in one row, all facing the same way, close enough that their shoulders nearly touch. Leave one empty place in the row, as wide as one man.

To bring out a model: press the top button on your right controller, click Models, and type the word in the Filter models box. Grab a model with the palm trigger (under your middle finger). Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

Step 4 of 7Send one man ahead

Bring out one Greek Mercenary Running. Set him about ten steps in front of the empty place, facing away from the line.

Step 5 of 7Read your own model

Stand in the empty place and look left and right at the men beside it.

Say it out loud

“When one man leaves the line, ___. The polis needed ___ because ___.”
Extra · VR only · if you have timeBring out a Spartan Helmet (type Spartan). Set it on the ground in front of the soldier whose courage you would rather have.

Step 6 of 7Take the photo and submit it

  1. Stand back until you can see the whole line, the empty place and the runner.
  2. Open your menu. Click Library, then Camera. Move the tablet until it all fits, then click Take photo.
  3. Click the Canvas tab and pick VR: Hold the Line.
  4. Click Choose from My Files, pick your photo, then click Use this file.
  5. Click Submit to Canvas. Look for the green Submitted tag.

The photo is the only thing you turn in. To fix something, move the models, take a new photo and submit again.

After

Step 7 of 7Check and leave

Press the Meta button on your right controller, then click Quit. Take off your headset and open the Canvas assignment. Your photo should be there. If it is not, open Having trouble in VR? below for two other ways to turn it in.

Full credit: Your photo shows five standing soldiers in one tight row with one empty place, and one running soldier alone in front of that place.

How-to video: Optima On-Demand and Canvas

First time only: connect Canvas (on your computer)

  1. 1Log in

    Go to xr.optimaed.com on your computer. Log in with the same login you use in VR.

  2. 2Connect to Canvas

    Click Connect to Canvas. Log in with your usual Canvas username and password, then click Authorize. You will see Canvas connection completed.

  3. 3Find our class

    Click Canvas on the left and pick our class. Our assignments show up. You only do this part one time.

In the headset

  1. 4Explore tab

    Put on your headset. Click the Explore tab and type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 5Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo.

  3. 6Read the assignment

    Press the top button on your right controller to open your menu. Click the Canvas tab and pick our assignment to read the directions. Tip: point at a mint side grip and squeeze to pull the tablet to your hand.

  4. 7Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type the exact name in the search box, or scroll with your thumbstick. Click a model to bring it out.

  5. 8Move and resize

    Grab a model with the palm trigger. Turn and move it with the thumbstick. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  6. 9Take a photo

    Click Library, then Camera. Move the tablet until your whole model fits. Slide Height or the zoom bar if you need to. Click Take photo.

  7. 10Pick your photo

    Click the Canvas tab and pick our assignment. Click Choose from My Files, pick your newest photo, then click Use this file.

  8. 11Submit to Canvas

    Click Submit to Canvas and look for the green Submitted tag. To leave VR, press the Meta button on your right controller, then click Quit.

Didn’t work in VR? Two other ways

  1. 12Use the website

    Go to xr.optimaed.com and click Canvas. Pick our assignment, click Choose from My Files, pick your photo and submit it.

Your photos save to My Files at xr.optimaed.com on their own. You can also download your photo there and upload it to the Canvas assignment yourself.
