B

The Task

Density is an intensive property — a fingerprint that can identify a substance. In this lab you’ll measure the density of an unknown material, use it to identify the material, and then do what a real scientist does with any result: evaluate how good your measurement was. You’ll judge precision and accuracy and compute your percent error.

You’ll write this up as a proper lab report in Microsoft Word. This is honors-level scientific writing: rigorous, evidence-based, and honest about its own limits.

Why this matters

This lab report is teacher-reviewed. Your teacher reads it personally and responds to your reasoning — so make it your careful best.

D

Percent Error

percent error = ( |experimental − accepted| ÷ accepted ) × 100%

Your experimental density is what you measured; the accepted density is the reference value for the material you identified. A small percent error means high accuracy. Keep this separate from precision — how closely your individual trials agree with one another.

Error & Uncertainty

8

CER Conclusion
