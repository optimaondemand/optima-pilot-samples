      The Quick Write 1 prompt

      Revenge or Forgiveness? When someone has wronged you and you could get even, which takes more strength: holding on, or letting go? Choose one person who faced that choice: a character from a story you know, or yourself. Name the moment, take a side, and support it with one specific detail. Write 150 to 200 words, an argument, not a summary. Use at least one of this week’s ten words.
