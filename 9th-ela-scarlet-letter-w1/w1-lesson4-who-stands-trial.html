Three Lenses

Claim
  • Is it clear whose reading of the letter you describe?

  • Is the quotation exact, with its chapter cited like this: (Ch. II)?
  • Does the sentence after it explain what it shows?
Sentences
  • Does one sentence open with a participial phrase, and does the subject follow right after the comma?
  • Does one semicolon join two halves that balance?
