Instructions

You have read the text of the Sedition Act of 1798 and studied how it was used. Write a report of 300 to 450 words. Use your own words except where you quote.

Part 1 · What the law does

In two or three sentences, say what the Act made a crime and what the penalty was. Quote one short phrase from the law's text.

Part 2 · Fair on paper?

Find one feature of the text that Andrew Hamilton, Zenger's lawyer, would have welcomed, and one feature that he would have objected to. Explain each.

Part 3 · Fair in use?

Use the lesson to say who was tried under the Act, and what that tells you about how a law can be fair on paper and unfair in use.

Part 4 · What this source cannot tell you

Name one question about how people at the time felt about the Act that the text alone cannot answer, and say where you might look for the answer.
