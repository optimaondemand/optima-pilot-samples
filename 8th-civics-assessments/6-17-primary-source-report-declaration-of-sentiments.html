Instructions

You have read the Declaration of Sentiments and studied how women won the vote. Write a report of 300 to 450 words. Use your own words except where you quote.

Part 1 · What it claims

In two or three sentences, say what the writers at Seneca Falls were asking for and what they said was wrong.

Part 2 · How it is built

The writers copied the shape of another famous document. Quote two short phrases from the Declaration of Sentiments that show this copying, and explain what the writers gained by borrowing from the Declaration of Independence.

Part 3 · Connect it to the lesson

The 19th Amendment passed seventy-two years after Seneca Falls. Name two things the lesson says people did during those years, and explain what the delay tells you about how the republic changes its rules.

Part 4 · What this source cannot tell you

Name one question about the movement that this document alone cannot answer, and say where you might look for the answer.
