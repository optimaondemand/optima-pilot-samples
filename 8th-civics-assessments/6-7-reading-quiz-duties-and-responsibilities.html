READING QUIZ
Reading Quiz: Duties and Responsibilities: Jury, Draft, and Virtue
5 questions · 5 points · 2 attempts
Before you start

Five questions on what the law requires of citizens and what a free republic hopes they will choose to do.

QUESTION 1
A man pays his taxes and registers for Selective Service. He never votes and never reads about problems in his town. Which description fits him best?
A. He has broken the law.
B. He is doing everything a good citizen should do.
C. He keeps his obligations but neglects his responsibilities.
D. He has lost the rights of a citizen.
QUESTION 2
Why does the lesson say jury service shows what a citizen really is?
A. It lets ordinary citizens share directly in ruling by deciding the facts of a case.
B. It is the one duty that people who are not citizens also owe.
C. It takes the place of voting for people who are called.
D. It gives lawyers and judges a break from their work.
QUESTION 3
Washington could have stayed President for life, but he stepped aside after two terms. What does the lesson want you to take from this?
A. He expected to lose the next election.
B. The Constitution barred a third term in 1796.
C. He was too ill to continue.
D. A republic needs leaders who can walk away, and no law forced him to do it.
QUESTION 4
Washington called religion and morality "indispensable supports" of a free government. According to the lesson, what did he think would happen if a people lost honesty and the habit of keeping promises?
A. They would need no government at all.
B. They would need a strong ruler to keep order, and then they would no longer be free.
C. They would grow richer, because they would no longer follow rules.
D. They would be protected by the courts from the results.
QUESTION 5
A friend says, "If it isn't against the law, it isn't my business." Which idea from the lesson answers him best?
A. Laws apply only to citizens who were born here.
B. Responsibilities are only for people who plan to run for office.
C. Responsibilities are the true test of character, because when you ignore one, only the community suffers.
D. A citizen who obeys the law has already done everything a republic needs.
