Moving Between Segments

One sentence that carries the viewer from each segment to the next. Six short lines, one per hand-off, such as "So that is the land. Now the people who worked it."

  • Every claim in the script has a source in my log.

Rehearsal Checklist

  • I speak from key lines, not by reading a page aloud.
  • I say the name of each factor out loud when I reach it.
  • I point at the one thing to notice on each chart.
  • I stop for a breath between segments.

Words

Subject vocabulary
IncentiveA reward or reason that pushes people toward one choice over another.
Opportunity CostThe value of the next-best choice you give up when you decide.
ScarcityThe condition of having limited resources while human wants have no limit.
Trade-OffGiving up one thing in order to get another.
EntrepreneurshipThe drive to start something new, take risks, and combine land, labor, and capital into a working business.
Human CapitalThe skills, knowledge, and experience that make workers more productive.
Physical CapitalHuman-made tools, such as machines, buildings, and roads, that are used to produce goods.
ThesisOne sentence that states your answer and that someone could argue against. The rest of the project proves it.
StoryboardA plan of the slides in order, with what you say and what the viewer sees on each one.
Source LineA short citation in the corner of a slide, such as (Last, Year), that points to the full entry on the last slide.
Reference ListThe full APA entries for every source you used, listed alphabetically on the last slide.
