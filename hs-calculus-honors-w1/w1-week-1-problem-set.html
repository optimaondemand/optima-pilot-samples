Week 1 Problem Set: Limits from Graphs and Tables

Focus

Can a limit exist where the function itself is undefined? Estimate limits from tables and graphs, apply the limit laws, and explain your reasoning.

Objectives
  • Estimate a limit from a two-sided table of values.
  • Evaluate limits by direct substitution and by the limit laws, naming the rule you used.
  • Read a limit and a function value from a graph, and say why they can differ.
  • Explain why f(a) does not decide limx→a f(x).

Type answers as numbers: 5.25, -3, or a fraction like 7/4. Show your steps and your reasoning in the written boxes.
