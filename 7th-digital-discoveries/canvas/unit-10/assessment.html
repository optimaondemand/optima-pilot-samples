Unit 10 · Assessment · Capstone

Show What You Know — and What You Made

The year's final measure is the work itself.

Two parts

  1. Unit 10 Quiz — a short auto-graded quiz on how the web works, creating online content with design tools, and the careers this year has touched. Take it in Canvas when your teacher opens it.
  2. The Capstone Portfolio — the largest single grade of the year — submitted through the Capstone Portfolio assignment and counted in the Projects portion of your grade: your published portfolio, your 2–4 minute recorded presentation, and your final written reflection. Read that assignment's requirements and rubric before you finish.
How the capstone is scored

The rubric rewards: a curated portfolio (welcome + 4–6 artifacts, each introduced with the skill it shows and a lesson learned); clean, accessible design (headings, contrast, alt text); a recorded presentation in your own voice with honest reflection; and credit given for tools and sources.

Before you submit Open your portfolio one last time as a stranger would. Is it good (well made), true (honestly yours), and beautiful (designed with care)? Then it is ready.
