Unit 10 · Lesson 3

Gathering the Year

A portfolio is not a pile. It is a story you choose to tell.

Curating your portfolio

A digital portfolio is a curated collection of your work, published as online content. Curated is the key word: a museum shows its best hundred paintings, not its whole basement. Your capstone portfolio needs:

  • A welcome page — who you are as a maker, in a few honest sentences.
  • 4–6 artifacts from across the year — chosen because each shows a different skill.
  • An introduction for each artifact — what it is, what skill it shows, and one thing you learned making it (this reflection is where the real growth shows).
  • Credit where due — name the tools you used and cite anything you built upon.

What you have to choose from

Look back — you have made more than you think: an algorithm ordered by hand, Boolean rules, traced programs, your Scratch build, a simulation investigation, VR builds, discussion posts that changed someone's mind. The next activity helps you see which skill each artifact proves.

Hands-on: What does each artifact prove?

Match each artifact from your year to the skill it best demonstrates — a museum label for your own work.
