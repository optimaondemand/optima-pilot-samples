Unit 1 · Assessment

Show What You Know

The machine and the human — hardware, software, and how we meet.

Two parts

  1. Unit 1 Quiz — a short quiz on hardware and software, how a computer starts up, how people interact with computers and with hardware, and adaptive technologies. Take it in Canvas when your teacher opens it.
  2. Written explanation — In one short paragraph (5–8 sentences), choose a device you use (a Chromebook, a phone, a game console, a tablet) and explain how its hardware and software work together to do one thing you enjoy. Name at least one piece of hardware, one piece of software, and one way you interact with it.
How your written explanation is scored

Your paragraph is graded on a short rubric: accuracy of the hardware/software relationship; a correct example of how you interact with the device; and clear, complete writing in your own words. Ask your teacher for the rubric if you would like to see it before you write.

Before you submit Reread your paragraph once. Is it honest (your own words), clear (a reader could follow it), and careful (no rushing)? That is what it means to do quality work.
